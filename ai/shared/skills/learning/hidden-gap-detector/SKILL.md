# Hidden Gap Detector

Diagnose the learner's mental model without teaching all the answers first.
Run this procedure independently; do not invoke existing quiz or critique skills.

## Ground the diagnostic

Identify the skill, its scope, and the claimed level. Ask for missing scope before
testing. When the learner names a codebase, inspect actual implementation and tests
and use verified source locations in feedback. Otherwise use supplied material and
reliable subject knowledge, stating uncertainties. Distinguish code behavior from
ideal design or general theory.

Prepare five short, apparently straightforward questions that expose different
foundational gaps. Favor causal reasoning, boundary conditions, counterexamples,
prediction, and transfer. Each question needs an expected mechanism and a fair
criterion; obscure trivia and arbitrary jargon do not demonstrate depth.

## Ask and diagnose

Ask one question at a time, request the answer and reasoning, then stop. Do not
show later questions, the answer key, or suggestive multiple-choice distractors.
Optional confidence ratings can expose overconfidence but do not determine scores.

For each answer:

1. Quote the consequential claim accurately.
2. Classify it as sound, incorrect, right for the wrong reason, incomplete, or not
   yet assessable from the answer.
3. Explain what it reveals about the foundation, with a concrete contrasting case
   and source evidence where relevant.
4. Ask one focused follow-up when ambiguity prevents a fair diagnosis.

Be direct and specific, never insulting. Do not invent a flaw to satisfy “prove me
wrong.” If an answer is strong, say exactly why and move on. Accept valid alternative
approaches. Separate optional sophistication from knowledge necessary for the stated
level. Do not claim later answers were unaided if earlier feedback taught that point.

## Final gap map

After five questions, summarize demonstrated strengths and gaps ranked by their
practical consequence. For each gap give one targeted exercise and what success
would look like. Distinguish lack of knowledge from imprecise communication, and
state which conclusions remain uncertain. A clean diagnostic establishes no gaps
found within this sample, not exhaustive mastery. Offer a fresh transfer round only
if requested; honor stop/skip requests throughout. No project mutations are needed.
